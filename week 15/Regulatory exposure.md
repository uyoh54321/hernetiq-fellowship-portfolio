## Regulatory Exposure

| Finding | Regulation | Article | Regulatory Exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unverified external training data | EU AI Act | Article 10(2), (3) | Inadequate data governance, data quality, and bias examination for high-risk AI systems. |
| Automated deployment without human approval | EU AI Act | Article 14(1), (4) | Insufficient human oversight of high-risk AI systems. |
| Missing conformity assessment | EU AI Act | Article 43 | High-risk AI systems may be placed on the market or put into service without the required conformity assessment. |
| Cross-tenant data access | GDPR | Articles 5(1)(f), 32 | Potential breach of personal data confidentiality and security obligations, where personal data is involved. |
| Missing model performance monitoring | EU AI Act | Article 72 | Failure to establish required post-market monitoring for high-risk AI systems. |

> **Note:** EU AI Act requirements depend on the system's classification and the provider/deployer's role. GDPR applies where personal data is processed.
