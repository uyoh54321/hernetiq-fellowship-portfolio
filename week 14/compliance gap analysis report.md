# AI Compliance Gap Analysis Report

**Overall Risk:** HIGH

## 1. Executive Summary

The assessment identified three key gaps: unchecked external training data, missing bias reviews, and automated model deployment without human approval.

These gaps may lead to unreliable outputs, unfair decisions, and compliance risks.

## 2. Key Findings

### Finding 1: Unchecked Training Data
- **Evidence:** External data bypasses integrity and schema checks.
- **Risk:** Unreliable model outputs and costly errors.
- **Reference:** EU AI Act Article 10, where applicable.

### Finding 2: Missing Bias Review
- **Evidence:** The external dataset has no completed bias review.
- **Risk:** Unfair outcomes and reputational damage.
- **Reference:** EU AI Act Article 10, where applicable.

### Finding 3: Deployment Without Human Approval
- **Evidence:** Models are deployed automatically without human sign-off.
- **Risk:** Harmful decisions and operational losses.
- **Reference:** EU AI Act Articles 14 and 16, where applicable.

## 3. Recommended Actions

| Action | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Enforce data integrity and schema validation | CTO | 30 days |
| Require and document bias reviews | Compliance Lead | 30 days |
| Introduce human approval before deployment | CTO | 30 days |
| Confirm applicable legal obligations and verify remediation | Compliance Lead | 45 days |

## 4. Conclusion

Prioritize these gaps, implement corrective controls, and retain evidence of remediation for audit purposes.

Confirm EU AI Act applicability before treating any cited provision as a binding requirement.
