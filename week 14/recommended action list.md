## 4. Recommended Actions

| # | Recommended Action | Owner | Timeframe | Measurable Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify external training data before use. | CTO | 30 days | All external data passes validation. |
| 2 | Complete bias reviews before training and deployment. | Compliance Lead | 30 days | All relevant datasets have documented bias reviews. |
| 3 | Require human approval before production deployment. | Engineering Lead | 30 days | All production deployments have recorded approval. |
| 4 | Assess applicable regulations and document compliance gaps. | Legal/Compliance Lead | 45 days | Compliance assessment completed with assigned actions. |
| 5 | Keep records of data checks, bias reviews, and deployment approvals. | AI Governance Lead | 45 days | Centralized audit records are available for review. |

### Priority

Actions 1–3 address the main control gaps. Actions 4–5 strengthen regulatory compliance, accountability, and audit readiness.
